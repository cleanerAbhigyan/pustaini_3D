## Required fields for every future asset
Stable ID; relative path or canonical external URI; filename; type; dimensions/size; hash if applicable; creator/source URL; license/permission; approval status; observed vs generated vs mockup; associated product/scene; intended use; dependencies; notes. Do not claim a screenshot is an approved manufactured product image. Use `assets/references/` for research and `public/assets/` only for optimized approved assets used by the app.
